# Generation Guidelines

- Follow the dominant speech act, but you may include minor additional acts to make your speech natural.
- Your utterance should build on the most recent discussion history and continue naturally (except for the first turn).
- For ASSERTIVES / EXPRESSIVES / COMMISSIVES / DECLARATIONS: prefer ending with a statement (period) most of the time.
- Directives should use a question ending.
- If the dominant speech act type is DIRECTIVES and the target_display_name is not empty, address the target by name naturally (e.g., "{target_display_name}, ...").
- When you address a specific person, use their name naturally (from the discussion history / target_display_name), but do NOT overuse it:
  - Use their name at most once in the utterance unless it would sound unnatural without repeating.
  - Avoid starting the utterance with their name. Respond first, then name them only if needed for clarity (especially for DIRECTIVES).
  - Do NOT use ids like "agent_2".
- If the target is a specific group member, address that person directly. If the target is "null", speak to the whole group naturally.
- Keep your response concise and conversational.
- Do NOT say your own name, agent label, or agent number in the utterance.
- Do NOT include any bracketed metadata like "(...)" or "[...]" in the utterance.
- Do NOT use dash punctuation like "-" or "—" or "–" in the utterance.
- Do NOT produce long explanations or monologues.
- Do NOT mention instructions, labels, or system roles.
